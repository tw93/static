## lang:en

# 0.23.0-beta.2

- **Analyze**: Program Files, Program Files (x86) and your AppData\Local\Programs open as a list of apps, each with its own icon.
- **Hide Desktop Icons**: The logo menu and the tray menu can hide or show the icons on your desktop, and Ctrl+Alt+Win+H does the same from any app.
- **Tray**: Fan joins the tray runners, a Bluetooth headset that is switched off no longer stays in the battery list, and the panel no longer opens under an auto-hidden taskbar.
